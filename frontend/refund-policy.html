Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Boojee Cafe, we strive to ensure that every experience, whether in-person or online, meets our high standards. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, we’re here to help.

1. Coffee Beans and Perishables

Because coffee is a perishable product, we do not accept returns on roasted coffee beans or any baked goods. However, if there was an error with your order or the product arrived damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@boojeecafe.com, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

2. Merchandise and Brewing Equipment

We accept returns on unused, non-coffee items (such as mugs, apparel, and brewing equipment) within 14 days of the delivery date. Items must be returned in their original packaging and condition. The original shipping cost is non-refundable.

3. Processing Refunds

Once we receive your returned item, our team will inspect it and notify you of the status of your refund. If approved, the refund will be processed to the original method of payment within 5-7 business days.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions on how to return your item to us, please reach out to our customer care team.
