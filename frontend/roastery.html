HOW WE ROAST

The Six-Step Roast Method.

From estate-level green bean grading to nitrogen-sealed micro batches, here is how we ensure clarity in every extraction.

STAGE 01

Estate Sourcing & Grading

We work exclusively with certified single-origin growers across Chikmagalur, Shevaroys, and Araku Valley, selecting only high-altitude Grade AA beans.

STAGE 02

Thermodynamic Profiling

Each harvest is test-roasted through digital infrared sensors to calibrate airflow, drum velocity, and thermal charge temperatures.

STAGE 03

Developing the First Crack

As sugars caramelize and bean pressure releases, our roaster modulates conductive heat to preserve vibrant fruit acids and delicate aromatics.

STAGE 04

Rapid Aerated Cooling

Beans are discharged into an agitated cyclone cooling tray within 180 seconds, locking in nuanced flavour compounds immediately.

STAGE 05

Q-Grade Cupping Lab

Every single batch undergoes sensory evaluation for sweetness, body, acidity, and cleanliness before packaging.

STAGE 06

Nitrogen-Flushed Bags

Packaged with one-way degassing valves and airtight seals to guarantee cafe-fresh extraction for every cup.
