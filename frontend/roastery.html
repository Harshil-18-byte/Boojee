THE ROASTERY

Sourced with care. Roasted with intent.

Our approach to coffee is guided by seasonality, transparency, and a deep respect for the producers.

The Pursuit of Quality

At Boojee, roasting is about revealing the inherent characteristics of the coffee, rather than imparting the flavor of the roast itself. We meticulously profile each coffee to find the sweet spot—where acidity, sweetness, and body are in perfect harmony.

Sourcing Philosophy

We work closely with trusted import partners and directly with producers to source coffees that are not only exceptional in the cup, but also ethically and sustainably grown. We pay a premium for quality, ensuring that the farmers are fairly compensated for their hard work.

Small-Batch Roasting

We roast in small batches to maintain tight control over the development of the coffee. Our sensory team cups every roast to ensure consistency and quality before it ever reaches the hopper at the cafe or the shelf in the shop.
