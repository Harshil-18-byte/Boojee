Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit the Boojee Cafe website and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create or modify your account, request on-demand services, contact customer support, or otherwise communicate with us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. We also collect certain information automatically as you navigate through our digital services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect about you to provide, maintain, and improve our services. This includes processing transactions, sending electronic receipts, providing responsive customer support, and notifying you about technical updates or changes to our operations.

3. Data Security

We use state-of-the-art security measures to protect your data, including secure HttpOnly cookies for session management and encryption for passwords. Payments are securely processed via industry-standard providers (Stripe, Razorpay). We do not store raw credit card details on our servers.

4. Newsletter & Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we will use your email to send you updates regarding seasonal menus, small-batch releases, and cafe news. You may opt out of these communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link found at the bottom of every email.
