Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We are dedicated to delivering our small-batch roasted coffee to your door as fresh as possible. This policy outlines our shipping procedures, delivery times, and costs.

1. Order Processing

All coffee orders are roasted to order and dispatched within 48 hours of purchase to ensure maximum freshness. Orders placed after 12 PM on Fridays or during weekends will be processed on the following Monday.

2. Delivery Options

We offer standard and expedited shipping options at checkout. Standard shipping typically takes 2-4 business days, while expedited shipping takes 1-2 business days. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order has been dispatched.

3. International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within the UK. We are actively working on expanding our delivery network to reach our international customers soon.

4. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the weight of your order and your location. We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders over £40.
